Who We Are & What We Do

WHO WE ARE: Vidyarthi Motors Private Limited is an ISO 9001:2015 certified automotive clean-tech innovator headquartered in Haldwani, Nainital, Uttarakhand. Backed by over 5 years of commercial field testing (2019–2025), we hold granted international patents protecting active air treatment for heavy diesel internal combustion engines.

WHAT WE DO: We design, manufacture, and install patented Active Air Enhancement Technology (FST) units. By modifying the stock air intake manifold with a capacitive discharge wire-mesh matrix powered by 12V/24V DC, our retrofits enrich intake airflow with oxygen—compensating directly for EGR oxygen depletion, restoring full volumetric efficiency, and delivering 14% to 33% fuel savings with zero engine modification.

What It Is: Patented Active Air Enhancement

Our patented capacitive technology enriches intake air with electrical oxygen excitation, addressing the core oxygen starvation challenges of Exhaust Gas Recirculation (EGR) directly at their source.

By elevating internal atmospheric energy, it raises the Vrms velocity and kinetic energy of incoming intake air. This restores diesel engine volumetric efficiency, delivering complete combustion, higher piston torque, and greater indicated power output—all while slashing black smoke emissions by 50%.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know About FST

Answers to key technical, operational, and commercial questions for fleet managers and vehicle operators.

Exhaust Gas Recirculation (EGR) cuts NOx by sending inert exhaust back into the engine intake, which deprives the combustion chamber of vital oxygen. FST uses a patented conducting wire-mesh capacitive discharge matrix inside the intake manifold. Powered by 12V/24V DC, it electrically charges incoming air molecules, compensating for lost oxygen and accelerating volumetric burn efficiency. This results in fuller combustion, higher piston torque, and reported fuel savings between 14.14% and 33.34%.

No. FST is a 100% plug-and-play retrofit unit. It directly replaces the stock air intake inlet tube and manifold coupler. It requires zero internal engine drilling, zero cylinder head changes, and zero ECU remapping. If required, the vehicle can be returned to factory stock condition at any time.

For a medium or heavy commercial truck covering 300 km daily (consuming ~85 litres of diesel daily at ₹95/L), a 20% fuel saving saves approximately ₹5,81,400 annually. With an estimated FST device fitment cost between ₹75,000 and ₹1,00,000, fleet operators achieve full capital payback in just 1.5 to 2 months, generating over ₹29+ Lakhs in net savings per vehicle over 5 years.

FST has been field-tested for over 5 years across major commercial platforms including Tata BS-III/BS-IV (1512 series, 2818 series, 5523 series), Ashok Leyland BS-IV engines, and 6-cylinder Cummins powerplants. It is also engineered for agricultural tractors, construction excavators, marine diesel auxiliary units, and base diesel gensets.
