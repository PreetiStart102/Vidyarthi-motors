About Us

Active Air Technology

Vidyarthi Motors Private Limited is an incubated clean-tech startup at TBIF, IIT Ropar that has engineered a patented combustion optimisation retrofit (Fuel Saving Technology - FST). By modifying the air intake inlet tube with a wire mesh capacitive discharge system, it enriches intake air with oxygen, counteracting EGR oxygen starvation at its source.
