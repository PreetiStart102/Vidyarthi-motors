Technical Workflow

How Active Air Enhancement Works

4-stage active air enhancement process restoring engine volumetric efficiency and power output.

Step 01
Oxygen Charge

Charges intake air with oxygen via conducting wire mesh capacitive discharge.

Step 02
EGR Compensation

Compensates directly for severe oxygen depletion caused by Exhaust Gas Recirculation.

Step 03
Volumetric Efficiency

Raises kinetic energy & Vrms speed of air inside the engine intake manifold.

Step 04
Complete Combustion

Delivers higher piston torque, more indicated power, and cleaner emissions.

Technical FAQ

Product & Fitment Questions

Common technical questions regarding Active Air Enhancement FST retrofits, engine compatibility, and field results.

FST Active Air Enhancement Technology utilizes a conducting wire-mesh capacitive discharge matrix positioned directly in the intake airflow path. Energized by 12V/24V DC, it electrically excites atmospheric air molecules, increasing internal energy and Vrms air speed to compensate for EGR oxygen starvation.

Yes. FST is compatible with BS-III, BS-IV, and BS-VI diesel engine platforms including Tata 1512, 2818, 5523 series, Ashok Leyland commercial engines, Cummins 6-cylinder platforms, agricultural tractors, excavators, and industrial diesel gensets.

Zero risk. FST is a non-invasive bolt-on retrofit that replaces stock air intake tubing without modifying internal engine components, fuel injectors, or ECU programming.
